# Education Data

`schools_teachers_enrollment_1956_1980.csv` — schools, teachers, enrolment and related indicators for El Salvador, compiled from published secondary sources (November 2026). One row per year × indicator × level, with the source, the original source the author cites, notes and a confidence flag.

**Status:** a provisional backbone. Teacher headcounts by year are not yet available; the best sources for them are the *Memorias de Labores del Ministerio de Educación* and the annual Ley de Salarios in the Diario Oficial (e.g. Ley de Salario: Ministerio de Educación, DO Tomo 209, n° 236, 23 Dec 1965, pp. 13359-13400).
