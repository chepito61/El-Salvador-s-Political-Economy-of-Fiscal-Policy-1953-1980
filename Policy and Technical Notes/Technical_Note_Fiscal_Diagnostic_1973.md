# El Salvador: Fiscal Diagnostic and Vulnerability Assessment, 1973

**Technical Note — A Coffee Boom Clears the Central Bank, Narrows the Deficit and Finances a Debt-Led Works Programme (November 2026)**

> **About this note.** This is the fiscal diagnostic for 1973. It follows the structure of the diagnostic series for 1952–1972.
>
> **Sources.** The *Informe Complementario Constitucional* 1973:
> - the analytical narrative (Cuadros 1–24);
> - Estado I-4 (revenue by item: coffee duty and income-tax split);
> - Estado I-6 (gastos by unit and class);
> - debt statements VII-1 and VII-2.
>
> Ministry totals are from Cuadro 8 (in thousands; sums exactly), with the main ramos checked on Estado I-6. Every figure was read on the page image and checked by arithmetic.
>
> **Pillars.** Development = education + health + public works. Security = Defensa + Ramo de Seguridad Pública.

---

## Executive Summary

**The fiscal position is assessed as "rising external debt, easing deficit, moderate vulnerability (cyclical)".**

- **Revenue.** Current revenue +23.4% to ₡402.7M, 11.1% above estimate, the largest increase in the series.
  - Coffee price +23.8%; export duties +60.3%; coffee duty ₡74.0M.
  - Income tax +20.2%; company income tax ₡20.5M (₡11.6M in 1970).
  - Tax / GDP 11.8% (Memoria), the highest of the period.
- **Spending.** Gastos +19.4% to ₡401.6M; credits used +17.3% to ₡450.0M.
  - Public works +66%; agriculture +39%; University current funding +59% (under government administration).
  - Teachers +5.8%; Fuerza Armada +31%; Presidency lump sum +77%.
- **Deficit.** Commitment basis −₡47.3M (−11.7%). **Underlying −₡8.7M (−2.2%);** in surplus before amortization.
- **Liquidity.** Superávit Financiero ₡39.1M. **Central Bank notes repaid: ₡17.4M → ₡0.7M.**
- **Debt.** Direct debt +25.6% to ₡258.3M (64% of revenue), almost all external. Service 5.1% of revenue. Total public debt 14.4% of GDP.
- **Composition (gastos):** development 49.7% (+25%); security 9.2% (+11%); ratio 5.4x, the highest in the series.

Currency: US$1.00 = ₡2.50.

## 9. Assessment

**Overall: rising external debt, easing deficit, moderate vulnerability (cyclical).**

- **The coffee boom produced the best fiscal year of the decade.** The underlying deficit fell to 2.2% of revenue; the Central Bank was repaid; the financial surplus reached ₡39M.
- **The windfall was spent on works and agencies, not on wages.** Public works, agriculture and the University grew fastest. Teachers' pay grew 6%.
- **Revenue became more dependent on coffee** (18% of revenue), and the debt more external. Both raise the cost of the next downturn.
- **Company income tax began to respond,** nearly doubling since 1970. This is the first sign of movement on track A, but it remains small next to the coffee export duty.
- **Opacity at the top grew.** The Presidency's lump sum in non-personal services nearly doubled.
