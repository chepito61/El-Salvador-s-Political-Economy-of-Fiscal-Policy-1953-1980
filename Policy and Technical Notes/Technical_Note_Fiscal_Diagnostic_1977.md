# El Salvador: Fiscal Diagnostic and Vulnerability Assessment, 1977

**Technical Note — A Coffee-Peak Surplus, Treasury Lending, and Rising Security Spending (November 2026)**

> **About this note.** This is the fiscal diagnostic for 1977. It follows the structure of the diagnostic series for 1952–1976.
>
> **Sources.** The *Informe Complementario Constitucional* 1977:
> - the analytical narrative (Cuadros 1–23);
> - Estados I-4 and II-1 (revenue, 1973–1977);
> - Estado IV-2 (spending by unit, **1976 budget liquidated at end-1977**).
>
> The volume has **no debt statement by instrument.** Ministry comparisons are on credits used (Cuadro 9). In 1977 Hacienda absorbs Deuda Pública and Transferencias Generales. Every figure was read on the page image and checked by arithmetic.
>
> **Pillars.** Development = education + health + public works. Security = Defensa + Ramo de Seguridad Pública.

---

## Executive Summary

**The fiscal position is assessed as "surplus at the coffee peak; Treasury liquid and lending; revenue at maximum concentration; security spending surging; very high political vulnerability".**

- **Revenue.** Current revenue +45.9% to ₡1,181.8M.
  - Coffee duty ₡416.8M (+83.5%), **35.3% of revenue.**
  - Income tax ₡164.2M (+17.8%); company income tax ₡55.9M (+7.5%).
  - Tax / GDP: 16.6% (World Bank, 1979); MH 1977 revises 1976 to 12.1%.
- **Spending.** Credits used +20.8% to ₡1,241.9M; gastos +13.6% to ₡915.3M.
  - Defensa +39.5%; Seguridad Pública +42.0%; Interior +100%; public works +27.7%; education +23.2%; health +14.6%; agriculture −10.3%.
  - Remunerations +16.8% (about +4.5% real).
- **Balance.** Commitment basis −₡60.1M (−5.1%). **Underlying +₡98.5M (+8.3%),** the first comparable surplus of the 1968–1977 series; about zero if the ₡100M lending fund is counted as spending.
- **Liquidity.** Treasury cash ₡137.2M; Central Bank advances repaid; floating debt −₡33.8M. Superávit Financiero ₡75.5M, still including ₡237M of unsold bonds (about −₡161M without them).
- **Debt.** Direct debt +7.1% to ₡466.2M (39.5% of revenue); guaranteed debt −2.0% to ₡461.3M; total public debt ₡927.5M (+2.4%).
- **Composition (credits used):** development 49.6%; security 7.5% (6.5% in 1976).

Currency: US$1.00 = ₡2.50.

## 9. Assessment

**Overall: surplus at the coffee peak; Treasury liquid and lending; revenue at maximum concentration; security spending surging; very high political vulnerability.**

- **The windfall was saved, late.** After spending most of the 1976 windfall, the government held credits used to +21% in 1977 while revenue rose 46%. The comparable balance swung by 19.5 points of revenue to a surplus.
- **The saving went into Treasury lending and cash.** The State repaid the Central Bank, cut arrears, and lent to its own enterprises (replacing guaranteed bonds) and to the coffee company.
- **The base for 1978 was fragile.** Revenue rested on one duty at a peak price; the wage bill and commitments were sized to that revenue; and the paper surplus still counted ₡237M of bonds no one had bought.
- **The tax bargain held.** The coffee boom's profits were taxed at the border; company income tax grew 7.5%.
- **The fiscal surplus coexisted with political closure.** Security spending grew 40% in the year of the fraud, the state of siege and the Public Order law.
