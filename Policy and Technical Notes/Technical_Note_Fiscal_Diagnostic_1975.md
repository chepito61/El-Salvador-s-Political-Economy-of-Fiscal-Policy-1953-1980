# El Salvador: Fiscal Diagnostic and Vulnerability Assessment, 1975

**Technical Note — Income Tax Up by Half, a Comparable Deficit of 10% of Revenue, and a Paper Surplus (November 2026)**

> **About this note.** This is the fiscal diagnostic for 1975. It follows the structure of the diagnostic series for 1952–1974.
>
> **Sources.** The *Informe Complementario Constitucional* 1975:
> - the analytical narrative (Cuadros 1–23);
> - Estado I-4 (revenue by item);
> - Estado IV-2 (spending by unit, **for the 1974 budget**);
> - debt statements VI-1 and VI-2.
>
> Ministry comparisons are on credits used (Cuadro 8). Every figure was read on the page image and checked by arithmetic.
>
> **Pillars.** Development = education + health + public works. Security = Defensa + Ramo de Seguridad Pública.

---

## Executive Summary

**The fiscal position is assessed as "structural deficit masked by a paper surplus; better tax mix; rising debt; high political vulnerability".**

- **Revenue.** Current revenue +19.1% to ₡576.8M, 20.8% above estimate, on a flat coffee price.
  - Income tax ₡118.9M (+48.6%), now the largest single tax; company income tax ₡47.3M (+46.6%).
  - Coffee duty ₡78.0M (−13.3%); a new sugar export duty ₡25.6M.
  - Tax / GDP 11.9% (Memoria, estimated GDP).
- **Spending.** Credits used +19.4% to ₡709.6M; gastos +17.7% to ₡599.6M.
  - Agriculture +46.5%; health +32.3%; public works +3.4%; Defensa +1.1%.
  - Remunerations +13.1%.
- **Deficit.** Commitment basis −₡132.8M (−23.0%). **Underlying −₡59.6M (−10.3%),** the widest since 1967.
- **Liquidity.** Superávit Financiero ₡101.6M, **built on ₡144.0M of unsold investment-plan bonds booked as receivable.** Without them, about −₡42M. Treasury cash fell from ₡27.1M to ₡9.3M.
- **Off-budget credit.** The General Fund lent ₡30.0M to the Compañía de Café, S.A.
- **Debt.** Direct debt +22.9% to ₡397.3M (68.9% of revenue); total public debt ₡663.8M (about 14.5% of GDP).
- **Composition (credits used):** development 48.3%; security 7.4%; social development (sectoral) 40.2%.

Currency: US$1.00 = ₡2.50.

## 9. Assessment

**Overall: structural deficit masked by a paper surplus; better tax mix; rising debt; high political vulnerability.**

- **The tax mix improved.** Income tax rose by half and a sugar duty taxed the sugar boom. Coffee's share of revenue fell to 13.5%.
- **The deficit did not.** Spending grew as fast as revenue, and the comparable deficit widened to 10.3% of revenue. The investment programme, mostly loan-financed, is now a fixed claim on the budget.
- **The financial surplus is not a cushion.** It rests on ₡144M of bonds that were authorized, booked as revenue receivable and not sold. Treasury cash fell by two-thirds.
- **The coffee company received ₡30M of Treasury credit** in the year its duty fell. It is the largest single loan on the General Fund's balance sheet.
- **Fiscal risk is now concentrated in the next coffee cycle.** The Brazilian frost of July 1975 would bring the 1976–77 windfall. The question for 1976 is whether it closes the structural deficit or finances more spending.
