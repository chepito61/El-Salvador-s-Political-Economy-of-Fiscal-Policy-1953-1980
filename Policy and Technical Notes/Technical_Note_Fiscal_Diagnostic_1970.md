# El Salvador: Fiscal Diagnostic and Vulnerability Assessment, 1970

**Technical Note — A Coffee Windfall Used to Clear the War Deficit, Old Arrears and the Food Agency's Losses (November 2026)**

> **About this note.** This is the fiscal diagnostic for 1970. It follows the structure of the diagnostic series for 1952–1969.
>
> **Sources.** The *Informe Complementario Constitucional* 1970:
> - narrative;
> - Estado II-5 (revenue);
> - Estado II-7 (gastos by unit and class);
> - debt statements.
>
> The 1970 and 1971 scans contain no Estado II-8, so **ministry comparisons are on the gastos basis** (II-7, 1969 and 1970). Every figure was read on the page image and checked by arithmetic.
>
> **Pillars.** Development = education + health + public works. Security = Defensa plus, from 1970, the new Ramo de Seguridad Pública.

---

## Executive Summary

**The fiscal position is assessed as "moderate debt, easing stress, moderate vulnerability (cyclical)".** The coffee boom restored the accounts. The underlying structure remained dependent on coffee.

- **Revenue.** Ordinary revenue rose 16.0% to ₡281.6M, 9.8% above estimate.
  - Coffee price +30.8%; coffee duty +62.6%.
  - Customs back to 40.3% of revenue (35.5% without the temporary CACM stabilization tax).
  - The income tax rose only 4.2%.
- **Spending.** Credits used +3.9% to ₡303.3M; gastos +9.0% to ₡269.5M.
  - Investment +67% in credits used, to ₡51.2M.
  - Payroll +6.0%, but primary teachers' pay only +3.6%.
- **Deficit.** Commitment-basis result −₡21.5M (−7.6%).
  - About 70% of it was settling old bills: ₡8.6M of social-security arrears, paid in land, and ₡7.3M of the IRA's past losses, paid in bonds.
  - **Underlying deficit −₡5.6M (−2.0%).**
- **Liquidity.** The Superávit Financiero went from −₡9.9M to +₡0.4M. Central Bank notes halved to ₡10M.
- **Debt.** Direct debt ₡137.6M (49% of revenue). War bonds ₡32.8M are two-thirds of domestic debt. Guarantees ₡191.6M (68% of revenue).
- **Composition (gastos):** development 47.6% (+8.1%); security 9.7% (−4.8%), now split between Defensa and the new **Ramo de Seguridad Pública**.

Currency: US$1.00 = ₡2.50.

**Assessment.** The war's direct cost in 1970 was small. Its financial legacy was paid out of the coffee windfall:

- ₡10M repaid to the Central Bank;
- the 1969 financial deficit of ₡9.9M absorbed by the operating saving.

The war bonds (₡32.8M) remain the largest block of domestic debt.

## 9. Assessment

**Overall: moderate debt, easing stress, moderate vulnerability (cyclical).**

- **The coffee boom allowed the State to clean its books.** It erased the war deficit, halved the Central Bank notes, settled social-security arrears in land, took over the food agency's losses and raised investment by two-thirds.
- **The structure did not change.** Revenue became more concentrated in coffee. The income tax shrank as a share. Industrial exemptions kept customs below their 1966 level. The Memoria itself warns of the "relative stagnation" of revenue since 1965.
- **The windfall bypassed the teachers.** Primary teaching pay rose 3.6%, against 15% for the University and 24% for the Presidency's discretionary lines. That was the setting of the 1971 strike.
- **Security was reorganized.** It shrank slightly in money, but the police got a ministry of their own.
