# War and Security Data

`war_cost_1968_1980.csv` tracks the fiscal cost of the 1969 war with Honduras and of later security emergencies, year by year, as recorded in the Memorias de Hacienda. Categories:

- **military spending:** extraordinary and emergency credits beyond the ordinary Ramo de Defensa;
- **financing:** taxes, bonds and Central Bank credit raised to pay for it;
- **fiscal impact:** the budget result with and without the war;
- **carry-over:** commitments, notes and debt service passed to later years.

The series is updated with each annual note (Note_MH_YYYY, section "The Cost of the War") and will feed a final synthesis note on the cost of the war. Amounts are in colones (US$1 = ₡2.50).
