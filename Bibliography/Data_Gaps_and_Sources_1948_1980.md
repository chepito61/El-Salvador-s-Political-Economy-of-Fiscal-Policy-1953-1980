# Data Gaps and Where to Find Them, 1948–1980

**Working inventory — November 2026.** Compiled from the "Data gaps" sections of the technical notes (1952–1979), the open-questions table of the Workflow note and the gaps found while writing the notes. Priority: **1** = limits a main conclusion; **2** = needed for a hypothesis test; **3** = useful detail.

---

## 1. The gaps that matter most

1. **Missing Memorias and missing pages.** There are no Memorias for 1948–1951 or for 1980, and no note yet on the 1960 Memoria, which is in the repository. Several scanned volumes lack key statements, including spending by ramo for 1964–1966 and 1970–1971, the 1971–1972 revenue detail and the 1968 unit table.
2. **Macroeconomic series before 1960.** We have no nominal GDP or price index for 1950–1964, and no annual coffee prices or export volumes before 1960. Without them the 1950s cannot be expressed as shares of GDP or in real terms.
3. **The Diario Oficial.** The content of the laws behind the main fiscal choices is missing:
   - budget laws: teachers' posts and salary scales, the University subsidy, the Presidency and Defensa lump sums;
   - tax laws: the December 1978 enterprise incentives, the late-1974 measures, the 1969 emergency tax, the "other merchandise" export duty;
   - executive transfers into Defensa in 1979;
   - beneficiaries of the development funds.
4. **Who paid and who benefited.** The cost of the tax exemptions, the income tax split by sector, the holders of government bonds and Central Bank notes, and the recipients of on-lending programmes.
5. **Real wages and social outcomes.** Teachers' posts and pay per head, real minimum wages, and employment.

## 4. Suggested order of work

1. **Now, with what we have:** write the note on the 1960 Memoria, which is already in the repository.
2. **Online, no travel:**
   - CEPAL *Estudio Económico* and Central American surveys for 1950s GDP, prices and exports;
   - the Grilli–Yang coffee prices;
   - IMF Archives consultation reports;
   - World Bank reports.
3. **One request to the Ministerio de Hacienda** (library or UAIP): Memorias 1948–51 and 1980–81, and the statements missing from the scans.
4. **Diario Oficial, targeted:** the December budget and salary laws for a few test years (1965, 1971, 1977, 1978), the December 1978 tax incentives and the 1979 *acuerdos* on Defensa.
5. **BCR:** national accounts, CPI, coffee volumes, credit to government.
